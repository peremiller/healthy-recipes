Privacy notice

Updated November 7, 2026.

Your recipes, meal plan, pantry and grocery data are saved on your device. If you choose to sign in, the existing account-based cloud-sync service also stores that data for your account. Local generated food illustrations are bundled with the app. Custom image URLs contact the image’s host when displayed.

Nearby restaurant searches start when you choose Use my location and permit browser location access, or choose a public area from the selector. An existing location permission granted to this site is reused when you return. The search sends coordinates and radius to this app’s restaurant endpoint. It does not send recipes, health tags, saved plans or account details. The default official merchant directory is evaluated on this app’s server, without sending your coordinates to the merchants. If the owner configures Google Places, Google receives coordinates for nearby search.

The app does not save your location or restaurant results to your meal-plan data, cloud-sync payload or offline archives. Results are shown only for the current session and expire. Clear location removes the current search state. Hosting and data providers may process requests according to their own policies.

Google Maps features incorporate the Google Privacy Policy. Opening an external Maps, directions or restaurant link uses that destination’s service.
