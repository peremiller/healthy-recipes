Privacy notice

Updated November 7, 2026.

Your recipes, meal plan, pantry and grocery data are saved on your device. If you choose to sign in, the existing account-based cloud-sync service also stores that data for your account. Local generated food illustrations are bundled with the app. Custom image URLs contact the image’s host when displayed.

Nearby restaurant searches start when you choose Use my location and permit browser location access. The search sends your coordinates and search radius to this app’s restaurant endpoint and its active data provider. It does not send recipes, health tags, saved plans or account details. OpenStreetMap searches round the location to about 100 metres before contacting the public search service. A configured Google Maps provider uses coordinates for nearby search.

The app does not save your location or restaurant results to your meal-plan data, cloud-sync payload or offline archives. Results are shown only for the current session and expire. Clear location removes the current search state. Hosting and data providers may process requests according to their own policies.

Google Maps features incorporate the Google Privacy Policy. OpenStreetMap-related services have their own privacy policy; the public search host also provides its privacy information. Opening an external Maps, directions or restaurant link uses that destination’s service.
